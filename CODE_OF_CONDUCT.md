# Code of Conduct

bim-os follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarises it; the Covenant's full text is what applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in bim-os to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, also when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Respect confidentiality: do not share other people's models, project data or personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any chat or event run for bim-os) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainers. Until a dedicated address is set up, contact the repository owner, [khjabir](https://github.com/khjabir), through the email address on their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow it, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful, following the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
